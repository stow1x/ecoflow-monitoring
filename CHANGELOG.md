What each bump means for this project:

- **major** — a metric was renamed, removed or rescaled, an environment variable changed meaning,
  or the container's contract changed. Somebody's dashboard or alert stops working.
- **minor** — new metrics, new device coverage, new panels or alerts. Existing series keep their
  name, unit and scale.
- **patch** — a wrong value becomes right, a crash stops happening, documentation improves.
